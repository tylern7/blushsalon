Good to know

Appointments

All appointments must be confirmed by text 24 hours before the scheduled time. If you no-show, there is a $75 fee that must be paid before booking any future appointments.

Services

All products and hair services are non-refundable. Appointments for hair adjustments must be made within 5 days of the original appointment.

Clothing

Blush Hair Studio is not responsible for clothing that may get stained during your appointment. Please don't wear anything you can't replace or would be upset to get colour on.
